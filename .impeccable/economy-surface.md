Reporting hierarchy: portfolio cash, average cost, fills, fees, returns and benchmark values use the reporting currency. Native quotes/history keep their original units; recorded rates and timestamps make conversion inspectable. The server owns conversion and risk checks; agents receive labelled base prices for affordability and position limits. Benchmarks start at their first recorded valuation and include subsequent FX changes. Twelve Data direct-pair `exchange_rate` uses the existing Setup key, a 45-second cache and shared provider quota. GBp/GBX, ZAc and ILA normalize minor units. Missing, stale or wrong-pair FX blocks conversion; day fills require rates under two minutes and long fills under twenty minutes. Stablecoin pegs are never assumed. No separate FX conversion fee, dividends, taxes or corporate-action model is added. Primary references: [exchange-rate API](https://github.com/twelvedata/twelvedata-java/blob/main/docs/CurrenciesApi.md) and [minor currency units](https://support.twelvedata.com/en/articles/12383853-why-are-some-securities-quoted-in-smaller-currency-units).

Closed-market valuation safeguard: Global markets can value a holding on a closed exchange at its last close, up to seven days old, only when its provider retrieval timestamp (`observedAt`) is within twenty minutes and required FX is fresh within twenty minutes. Preserve the visible closed-market notice and Last close timestamp so a recent retrieval is never presented as a recent local trade. This permits open-market trades while another held market is closed; the trade target must still be open with quotes/FX under two minutes for day tests or twenty minutes for long tests.
